Delete your Trexy account

Trexy The Treasure Chest — Trexy Development Team

You can request deletion of your Trexy account and associated online data at any time, including without installing or opening the game.

How to request deletion

  1. Tap the button below to email our support team.
  2. In your message, include your Trexy player name (if known) and state that you want your account deleted.
  3. We will reply with steps to verify account ownership before processing your request. Do not send passwords or one-time login codes.

Request account deletion by email

If the button does not open your email app, send a message to trexydevelopmentteam@gmail.com with subject Trexy account deletion request.

Data covered by the request

After verifying ownership, we will delete the account and associated online player profile, player name, score records and leaderboard entries under our control, unless specific information must be retained for legal, security or fraud-prevention reasons. Where retention is necessary, we will explain what is kept and why. Data controlled independently by third-party authentication or advertising providers may require separate requests to those providers.

Submitting an email is a request, not an instant automated deletion. We will confirm when your request has been processed.
