Privacy Policy

Trexy The Treasure Chest
Effective date: October 23, 2026

Trexy Development Team (“we”, “us”) provides Trexy The Treasure Chest (the “Game”). This policy explains information processed when you use the Game, including online player features and advertising. Contact us at trexydevelopmentteam@gmail.com.

Information we process

When you use online features, the Game may process your chosen player name, account authentication identifiers, game scores, leaderboard position, and related game activity. Authentication providers may process the information you choose to share when signing in. Local gameplay preferences and progress may also be stored on your device.

Why we use information

We use this information to provide sign-in, maintain player accounts, save and validate scores, display leaderboards, prevent cheating and abuse, troubleshoot problems, and operate the Game.

Advertising

The Game integrates Google AdMob. When advertising is enabled, Google and its partners may process information such as advertising identifiers, IP address, device information, app interactions, and ad performance data to deliver, measure, and protect advertising, subject to applicable settings and consent requirements. Ad formats may include banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads. Advertising may be unavailable or disabled while our integration or app approval is pending. Learn more about Google's data practices at Google Privacy Policy.

Service providers and sharing

We use Supabase to support authentication and online game data, and Google services for advertising when enabled. These service providers process information as needed to provide their services. Public leaderboards may display your chosen player name and score to other players. We do not claim to sell player account information.

Consent and choices

Where required, the Game will request advertising privacy choices before requesting ads. Depending on your location and applicable law, you may be able to manage advertising consent through an in-app privacy options control. You can choose not to watch rewarded ads. You can contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of personal data associated with your account.

Retention and deletion

Online account and score information is retained while needed to operate the Game and for legitimate security or compliance purposes. To request deletion of your account and associated personal data, email trexydevelopmentteam@gmail.com with the subject “Trexy Account Deletion Request” and your player name. We may need to verify account ownership. Some information may be retained when legally required or necessary for security.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including encrypted network connections for supported online services. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, contact us so we can review and take appropriate action. The Game's age targeting and advertising settings are subject to our Google Play declarations.

International processing

Service providers may process information in countries other than your own, subject to their security and privacy protections and applicable law.

Changes

We may update this policy as the Game and its services change. Material updates will be reflected by updating the effective date on this page.
